About

A China-based travel connectivity comparison project.

ConnectChina Guide is being built to help international visitors choose practical mobile connectivity options for trips to China.

What we do

We compare travel eSIM and connectivity options using traveler-relevant criteria such as trip length, data usage, fair-use rules, hotspot needs, product availability and current provider terms.

Why China-based research matters

Many travel-connectivity pages simply summarize merchant websites. Our long-term aim is to add original testing from within China, including activation experience, real-world connectivity and common traveler failure points.

How recommendations work

We separate traveler utility from affiliate economics. A higher commission does not automatically make an offer a better recommendation. When reliable evidence is unavailable, we describe the limitation rather than inventing a conclusion.

Project stage

This site is currently in an early public research and partner-validation stage. Some provider links are intentionally marked as pending until affiliate approval and tracking are configured.
