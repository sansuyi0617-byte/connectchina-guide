Why this site exists

Connectivity in China has extra travel friction.

International visitors often need to decide what to install before departure, whether hotspot use matters, and how much data is actually necessary. Those are decision problems—not just price-comparison problems.

Our comparison principles

We compare providers using traveler-relevant factors: plan duration, data allowance, fair-use policy, hotspot support, recurring use case, public affiliate terms and product availability. Where we do not have reliable evidence, we label it as unknown rather than guessing.
