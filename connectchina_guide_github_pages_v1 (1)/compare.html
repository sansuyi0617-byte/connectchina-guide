Rule 1

User value before commission

If two offers are not equivalent for the traveler, we do not rank the higher-commission offer first merely because it pays more.

Rule 2

Unknown stays unknown

If we cannot verify a network, fair-use limit, payout term or other material fact, we avoid presenting it as confirmed.

Rule 3

Prices can change

Promotions move quickly. Once partner APIs or live feeds are available, current-price data can be added without changing our comparison framework.

Rule 4

Real-world testing matters

Our long-term goal is to add original China-based performance testing rather than relying only on merchant marketing claims.
